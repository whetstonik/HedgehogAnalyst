Where ISKP came from, and what they're doing.

I had not known at all about ISKP until this year. I had known that IS forms a loose network across the post Islamic world, with cells in Africa doing their part in bringing back the great Islamic empire, but I never heard of their affiliates in Central Asia. It turns out, that their affiliates ISKP are downplayed by several of the neighbouring governments as a "proxy" of one or the other, and wasn't taken seriously as it's own standalone group. Priyadarshini Baruah is an analyst in New Delhi who wrote a great article explaining that governments are quick to point fingers at one another, without actually considering their ideologies.

ISKP lines closely with Islamic State, working for a unified Islamic empire, which their work will one day attribute to. They fight their fight on the Central Asian front, so that when IS prevails throughout the post Islamic world, the merging of IS the Kharuson Province will be seamless.

They are against Taliban because the Taliban have been irresponsible and neglect their population, and the Taliban reject the IS. The Taliban's non cooperation with the IS, and the Taliban's extreme tribalism and sloppiness of forming government make them even more incompatible.

Their base was in Afghanistan, but the Taliban successfully drove them out. Before they were drawn out, their strategy was hitting international assets within Afghanistan to convince foreign governments (Mainly Russia) that the Taliban can't protect their assets in Afghanistan, and the Taliban need to be taken care of by the bigger players first in order for them to deal with ISKP and be rid of their attacks. Now that they are, for the most part, out of Afghanistan, the ISKP are now conducting terrorist attacks outside of Afghanistan, namely Russia and Pakistan, to convince those nations that the Taliban can't protect them from Afghanistan terrorism exports. This strategy is actually partially working in Pakistan, where Pakistan does believe that the first solution to their internal counter terrorism struggle is to deal with the Taliban first, however this is more so the TTK's and BLA's doing, while ISKP contributed a little bit.
